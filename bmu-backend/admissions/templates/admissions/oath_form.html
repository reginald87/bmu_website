Section B — Declaration

I, {{ applicant_name }}, being a person admitted to pursue a programme of study at {{ university_name }}, do hereby solemnly and sincerely declare as follows:

  1. That I will faithfully and honestly fulfil my obligations as a student of {{ university_name }}, and that I will obey and abide by the Statutes, Ordinances, Regulations, Rules and Orders of the University as may be made from time to time.
  2. That I will at all times maintain a high standard of discipline and moral conduct, and I shall not belong to, participate in, promote or support any secret cult, cult activity, gang or unlawful society, whether within or outside the campus.
  3. That I shall not engage in any form of violence, examination malpractice, examination misconduct, drug abuse, bullying, harassment, or any other act capable of bringing the name of the University into disrepute.
  4. That I will be diligent and devoted to my studies and shall not engage in any activity that may prevent me from completing my programme within the prescribed time.
  5. That I will promptly and honestly pay all fees and levies prescribed by the University, and that I will not defraud the University or any member of the University community.
  6. That all the information and documents I have provided in support of my application are true, complete and genuine, and that any false declaration or falsified document may lead to the withdrawal of my admission, disciplinary action and/or prosecution.
  7. That I will uphold the good name and image of the University and its motto, “{{ motto }}”, both within and outside the campus.

I make this declaration conscientiously believing the same to be true, and by virtue of the provisions of the Oaths Act, I make this declaration conscientiously believing the same to be true and correct.
